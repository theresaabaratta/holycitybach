Charleston Bachelorette Planning Checklist

What to book and when, where to stay, and how to get the group around. Built for weekends of six to sixteen people.

Updated October 13, 2026

Charleston books up fast in spring and fall, so the order you book things in matters more than anything else. Lock the rental first, then the boat and big dinner, then fill the gaps with bachelorette activities. Once the bookings are in, map them onto a weekend itinerary so everyone knows where to be.

On this page: Timeline Where to stay Budget Getting around What to pack

Planning timeline

4 to 6 months out

Lock the basics

  • Confirm the date with the bride. March through May and October are the busiest months.
  • Collect the guest list and a rough headcount.
  • Set a per-person budget before anyone books anything.
  • Book the rental. Large houses go first.

2 to 3 months out

Book the big pieces

  • Boat charter or a beach day setup.
  • The big dinner and a group brunch reservation.
  • Classes, tours and any private entertainment at the rental.
  • Send the group a cost breakdown and collect deposits.

1 month out

Fill in the details

  • Pick a theme, outfits and decorations.
  • Reserve a rooftop or bar section for the night out.
  • Book a photographer if you want one.
  • Draft the itinerary and share it.

1 week out

Confirm everything

  • Reconfirm every booking by phone or email.
  • Check the forecast and pick a rain backup for outdoor plans.
  • Send the final itinerary with addresses.
  • Arrange airport pickups and rides.

Where to stay

Pick based on what the weekend is built around: nights out downtown, or days on the beach.

Best for nightlife

Downtown

Walk to dinner, bars and sights. Rentals run smaller, so big groups may need two.

Bars within walking distance

Best for space

Mount Pleasant

Larger homes, Shem Creek nearby and quick access to the islands and downtown.

Best for a laid-back trip

Folly Beach

Beach-town base with a walkable strip of bars. Plan rides for downtown nights.

Compare Charleston beaches

Best for large groups

Isle of Palms

The biggest beach houses plus a resort option. Furthest from downtown.

What to budget for

Most groups split shared costs evenly and cover the bride's share. List these out before you set the per-person number:

  • Rental: the biggest line item. Split by nights, not just headcount.
  • Boat or beach day: charter, cooler, food and drinks.
  • Activities: classes, tours and any entertainment. See the full list of Charleston bachelorette activity ideas.
  • Meals: one big dinner, one brunch, groceries for the house.
  • Transportation: rideshares, a shuttle or a party bus.
  • Extras: decorations, matching outfits, gratuity.

Save money

  • Go in January, February, July or August for lower rental rates.
  • Make one meal a catered or cooked dinner at the house.
  • Use a payment app with a shared group to collect costs early.
  • Ask about group minimums before booking classes.

Getting around

  • Downtown is walkable, but sidewalks are uneven. Skip heels for daytime.
  • Rideshare is the easiest way between downtown, the beaches and Mount Pleasant. Expect waits on Saturday nights.
  • Pedicabs work well for short hops downtown at night.
  • For groups over ten, a party bus or private shuttle beats splitting into multiple cars.
  • Flying in? Charleston International (CHS) is about 20 minutes from downtown.

What to pack

  • Walking shoes plus going-out shoes
  • Sunscreen, hats and a light layer for evening breezes
  • Swimsuits and a cover-up for the boat
  • An umbrella or rain jacket in summer
  • Decorations, sashes and group outfits
