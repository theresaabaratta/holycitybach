Charleston Bachelorette Brunch Ideas

Brunch is practically a sport in Charleston. Here are the formats that work best for a bachelorette group.

Saturday or Sunday brunch is often the most relaxed meal of the weekend and the one with the best photos. Charleston kitchens lean on Lowcountry staples like shrimp and grits, biscuits and fried green tomatoes, and plenty of places pour mimosas by the carafe.

Where to brunch

These are the spots and services that fit a bachelorette crew, from a sit-down Southern table to a mimosa sail and a chef who comes to the house.

Southern classic

Poogan's Porch

72 Queen St, Charleston

Poogan's Porch has served Lowcountry cooking out of an 1891 Victorian house since 1976, with porch tables on two floors. Brunch runs Saturday as well as Sunday, which makes it one of the few classic downtown spots that works before a Saturday boat or beach day. Order the biscuits, pimento cheese fritters and shrimp and grits, and ask your server about the house ghost. Parties of six to ten book easily; bigger groups should call ahead.

Coffee morning

Sweet Palm Coffee & Food

471 King St, Charleston

For a lighter start, Sweet Palm Coffee & Food sits on King Street a short walk from Marion Square. It's a food-first cafe, so the group can split yogurt bowls, house-made cinnamon rolls and croissants while everyone picks a signature latte, a dirty chai or a matcha. Sending one person ahead? Order from the online menu and grab chicken caesar wraps for the cooler before heading to the marina.

Mimosa brunch on the water

Palmetto Breeze Sunday Brunch Sail

Shem Creek, Mount Pleasant

Combine brunch and the harbor on the Palmetto Breeze Sunday brunch sail, a two-hour cruise on a 55-foot catamaran with light brunch bites and mimosas by the glass. It leaves from the Shem Creek boardwalk, between Water's Edge and Mount Pleasant Seafood, so you can keep the morning going at the creekside bars afterward. For a bigger boat day, see our boat day guide.

Private chef at the rental

Hibachi & Private Chef Bookings

Comes to your Airbnb or rental house

Skip the reservation and let the chef come to you. Hibachef sets up a full hibachi grill in the backyard, patio or garage of your rental anywhere around Charleston and brings the food, the fire show and the sake toss, while your group covers tables, plates and BYOB. Cozymeal's From Hibachi to Plate sends a local hibachi chef to you as well. For a slower morning-after brunch at the house, Food Fire + Knives books Charleston private chefs online and handles the cooking and cleanup.

Brunch booking tips

Groups over eight should reserve two to four weeks ahead and ask about set menus, which make the bill easier to split. Sunday brunch fills up faster than Saturday in many spots, and some restaurants seat large parties only at specific times.
