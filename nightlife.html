Charleston Bachelorette Nightlife

From rooftop sunsets to dancing on Upper King, here is how to build the big night out.

Charleston nightlife is concentrated, which makes it easy for a group. Most of the bars, rooftops and dance spots sit along or near King Street, so you can move from dinner to drinks to dancing on foot. The best nights follow a simple shape: sunset rooftop, dinner, then Upper King.

Tips for a group night out

  • Make a dinner reservation for the whole group weeks ahead.
  • Call rooftops in advance. Many cap group sizes or need a reservation.
  • Matching shirts or sashes help bars spot your group and help you find each other.
  • Set a meetup spot and a ride-home plan before the first drink.
  • Pedicabs are a fun short-hop option between downtown stops.
