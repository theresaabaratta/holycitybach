Charleston Bachelorette Nightlife

Rooftop sunsets, dinner, then dancing on Upper King. How to build the big night out.

Updated October 13, 2026

Charleston nightlife is compact. Most bars, rooftops and dance spots sit on or near King Street, so a group can go from dinner to drinks to dancing on foot. The best nights follow one shape: sunset rooftop, dinner, then Upper King.

Plan the night out as the last Charleston bachelorette activity of the day, after a daytime activity and some downtime at the rental. Start the next morning with a recovery brunch.

On this page: Build the night Group tips Sample night

Build the night

Pick three or four stops. More than that and you lose people between bars.

Start here · 6:30 to 8 pm

Rooftop Sunset Drinks

Downtown rooftops look over the harbor and church steeples at golden hour. Call ahead. Many cap group sizes.

Fit it into the itinerary

Most popular · 10 pm to close

Upper King Bar Crawl

The densest stretch of bars in the city. Walk between spots and finish wherever the dance floor is.

Any night

Live Music

From small bar stages to bigger shows. Check the Charleston Music Hall calendar for your dates.

Dressier · Before the late stop

Cocktail Lounges

Speakeasy-style bars for a quieter round. Pair with a cocktail class earlier in the day.

Late night

Dance Clubs

DJ spots for groups that want to dance until close. Expect a cover and a line on Saturdays.

Stay in

Private Party at the Rental

Keep the night going at home with hired entertainment, games and a private bartender. Book it two to three months out.

Tips for a group night out

  • Reserve dinner for the whole group weeks ahead.
  • Matching shirts or sashes help you find each other in a crowd.
  • Set a meetup spot and a ride-home plan before the first drink.
  • Rideshares back to Folly, IOP or Mount Pleasant get slow after midnight. Book a shuttle if you're staying out there. See where to stay.
  • Pedicabs are the easiest short hop between downtown stops.
