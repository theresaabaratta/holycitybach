Charleston Bachelorette Party Ideas: A 3-Day Weekend Plan

A Friday-to-Sunday plan with one anchor activity per day and room to breathe. Copy it, swap pieces and send it to the group chat.

Friday: Arrive & Kick Off
  • 3:00Check in and decorate the rental
  • 5:00Welcome drinks and gift bags
  • 6:30Rooftop sunset toast downtown
  • 8:00Group dinner on the peninsula
  • 10:00Ghost tour or cocktail lounge
Saturday: The Big Day
  • 9:00Coffee and pastries
  • 10:00Private boat charter or sandbar trip
  • 2:00Late lunch near the marina
  • 4:00Rest, pool time, get ready
  • 7:30Big dinner reservation
  • 10:00Upper King night out or private party at the rental
Sunday: Brunch & Beach
  • 10:30Mimosa brunch
  • 12:30Rainbow Row and Battery photo walk
  • 2:00Beach afternoon on Folly or Isle of Palms
  • 5:00Shem Creek early dinner
  • 7:00Head home or one last nightcap

How to adjust it

Low-key group: swap the night out for a private chef dinner and a sip-and-paint at the rental.

Beach-based trip: stay on Folly or Isle of Palms, move the boat day to a creek charter nearby and keep one downtown night.

Two-day trip: combine Friday dinner with the rooftop, keep Saturday as written and skip Sunday's beach.

Every activity in this plan can be pulled from these Charleston bachelorette party ideas.

Before you go

  • Confirm boat, dinner and brunch reservations the week before.
  • Share a weather backup plan in the group chat.
  • Collect money for shared costs up front.
