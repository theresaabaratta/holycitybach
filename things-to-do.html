Charleston Bachelorette Activities

Daytime ideas that fill the hours between brunch and dinner, from classic Charleston sightseeing to hands-on group activities.

Updated October 13, 2026

Most Charleston bachelorette weekends are built around three anchors: a beach day or boat day, a big dinner, and a night out downtown. The open hours in between are where a good activity makes the trip. Everything below works for groups of six to sixteen.

Want something booked and handled for you, including private entertainment at the rental? Browse these bookable Charleston bachelorette activities. To see how it all fits together, start with the sample weekend itinerary.

On this page: Classic Charleston Hands-on activities How to fit it in

Classic Charleston

Low-cost sightseeing that doubles as your best photo ops.

Free · 1 to 2 hours · Morning

Rainbow Row & Battery Photo Walk

The pastel houses on East Bay and the oak-lined Battery are the go-to group photo backdrop. Go early for soft light and empty sidewalks.

See where it fits in the itinerary

About 1 hour · Any time

Carriage Tour

A guided one-hour ride through the historic district. Easy and scenic, and a good pick for the morning after a late night.

Pair it with a brunch spot nearby

Free · 30 minutes · Sunset

Pineapple Fountain at Waterfront Park

A quick, iconic stop with harbor views and swinging benches along the water. Works well right before dinner.

60 to 90 minutes · Evening

Ghost Tour

After-dark walking tours that mix local history with haunted stories. A fun pre-dinner activity on night one.

Where to go after the tour

Free to browse · 2 hours · Afternoon

King Street & City Market Shopping

Boutiques along King Street, plus sweetgrass baskets and local goods at the City Market. A good time to pick up matching outfits for the night.

Free · 45 minutes · Daytime

Angel Oak

The massive live oak on Johns Island is a short drive from downtown. Check hours before you go; it closes earlier than you'd expect.

Continue on to Folly or Kiawah

Hands-on group activities

Bachelorette ideas that get everyone doing something together. Book these ahead, especially March through June.

2 to 3 hours · Afternoon

Lowcountry Cooking Class

Learn shrimp and grits or a Southern dessert together, then sit down and eat what you made. Doubles as lunch.

About 2 hours · At a studio or your rental

Sip & Paint

Book a private studio session or bring an instructor to your rental. Everyone leaves with a keepsake from the weekend.

Tips for booking at-rental activities

60 to 90 minutes · Afternoon

Flower Bar or Bouquet Class

Build your own arrangements. A slower afternoon option that photographs well.

About 90 minutes · Early evening

Cocktail Class

A bartender-led session on Southern classics. Schedule it right before you head out so the night starts on a high.

Charleston bars for the rest of the night

2 to 4 hours · Day two

Group Spa Day

Book side-by-side treatments, or bring a mobile spa team to the rental. Best placed on the second day as a reset.

1 to 2 hours · Morning or afternoon

Bike or Pedicab Tour

Cover more of the peninsula in less time, with a guide pointing out the highlights. Easier on the feet than walking it.

How to fit it all in

Plan one classic stop and one hands-on Charleston bachelorette activity per day. More than that and the group runs out of steam before dinner.

  • Friday: arrive, ghost tour, dinner, a first night out.
  • Saturday: photo walk, bachelorette brunch, then a class or the boat in the afternoon.
  • Sunday: spa or a slow beach morning before checkout.
