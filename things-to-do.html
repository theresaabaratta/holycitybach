Charleston Bachelorette Party Activities

Daytime ideas that fill the hours between brunch and dinner, from classic Charleston sightseeing to hands-on group activities.

Most bachelorette weekends in Charleston come down to a few big moments: a boat or beach day, a big dinner and a night out. The hours in between are where the best memories usually happen. Here are the daytime activities that work well for groups of six to sixteen.

Classic Charleston

Free

Rainbow Row & Battery Photo Walk

The pastel houses on East Bay and the oak-lined Battery make the go-to group photo backdrop. Go early for soft light and empty sidewalks.

Details

Carriage Tour

A one-hour ride through the historic district with a guide. Easy, scenic and good for the morning after a late night.

Details
Free

Pineapple Fountain at Waterfront Park

A quick, iconic stop for photos with harbor views and swinging benches along the water.

Details

Ghost Tour

Charleston's after-dark walking tours mix history and haunted stories. A fun pre-dinner activity on night one.

Details

King Street & City Market Shopping

Boutiques along King Street plus sweetgrass baskets and local goods at the City Market.

Details
Free

Angel Oak

The massive live oak on Johns Island is a short drive and makes a peaceful stop on the way to Kiawah or Folly.

Details

Hands-on group activities

Lowcountry Cooking Class

Learn shrimp and grits or a Southern dessert together, then eat what you made.

Details

Sip & Paint

A private studio session or an instructor at your rental. Everyone leaves with a keepsake.

Details

Flower Bar or Bouquet Class

Build your own arrangements. Great for a softer afternoon and pretty photos.

Details

Cocktail Class

A bartender-led session on Southern classics before you head out for the night.

Details

Group Spa Day

Book side-by-side treatments or bring a mobile spa team to the rental.

Details

Bike or Pedicab Tour

Cover more of the peninsula in less time, with a guide pointing out the highlights.

Details

How to fit it all in

Pick one classic stop and one hands-on activity per day. Pair the photo walk with brunch on Saturday morning, then book the class or spa for the afternoon so everyone has time to rest before dinner.

For bookable experiences, including private entertainment for the rental, browse these Charleston bachelorette party activities.

Good to know

  • Wear comfortable shoes. Historic sidewalks are uneven.
  • Book classes for groups ahead of time, especially in spring.
  • Carry water in summer. Midday heat is real.
